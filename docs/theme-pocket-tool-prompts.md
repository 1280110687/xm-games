# Pocket tool artwork prompts

Built-in ImageGen. Existing `documents.webp` and `tiles.webp` were used as style references only. Five new raster assets were generated independently, then resized to 320 × 320 WebP, quality 85. The existing transparent `documents.webp` is reused for text cleanup. Original PNGs remain in the ignored `output/theme-pocket-tool-assets/`.

The QR illustration is decorative and does not contain a functional data-sharing code. Tool logic and generated user QR codes are unchanged.

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one square tool launcher cover displayed at 48 to 64px, crisp raster illustration. The references show STYLE ONLY, do not copy their subjects. Match the existing XM-Games Pocket game's premium playful solid 3D toy aesthetic, glossy ceramic and resin materials, rich soft colors, thick rounded bevels, soft upper-left studio lighting and gentle contact shadows. NEW tool subject specified below. Full bleed softly colored background edge to edge, no outside rounded mask. Simple large central object occupies 80% of frame, readable small. Slight elevated three-quarter camera. No UI screenshots, labels, titles, logos, watermarks, people, fine print, flat vectors or line icons. Subject:

## anime-tracker.webp

A chunky glossy cobalt-blue mini television with a softly lit pale screen bearing one raised white play triangle on a blue screen inset. Small coral-red bookmark tab at its top corner suggests a saved watchlist. No characters or scene on screen. Two small rounded antennas. Light lavender-blue tabletop background. Simple inviting television, not a real branded product.

Production: `public/images/theme-pocket/anime-tracker.webp`

## text-crypto.webp

A large glossy golden padlock with thick rounded shackle standing in front of two layered ivory document sheets. A simple keyhole on the lock face, just three raised blue abstract text bars on the sheets, no actual writing. Cobalt-blue paper edges, warm cream tabletop background. Emphasize the protective lock as the single clear focal object.

Production: `public/images/theme-pocket/text-crypto.webp`

## qr-code.webp

A thick ivory square ceramic plaque bearing a stylized dark navy QR pattern, with three large square finder markers at top left, top right and bottom left, a few broad dark raised blocks elsewhere. Decorative QR only, must not encode data or URL. A glossy mint-teal rounded square frame and pale mint tabletop background. One plate with generous empty margin; no extra objects, no scanning UI.

Production: `public/images/theme-pocket/qr-code.webp`

## json-tool.webp

Two large thick glossy cobalt-blue curly braces, exactly { and }, standing either side of three small neatly stacked golden and ivory cubes in the center. Clear readable paired braces enclosing structured blocks, no letters or other punctuation. Soft lavender tabletop background. Tight simple sculptural composition of solid ceramic punctuation, not printed text or a code editor.

Production: `public/images/theme-pocket/json-tool.webp`

## base64-tool.webp

One large thick glossy cobalt-blue rounded square tile with the exact large raised white digits 64 on the front, flanked by two much smaller ivory chips showing a blue 0 and 1. A small curved golden physical arrow wraps partly around the main tile to suggest conversion. Pale cool blue tabletop background. Large 64 is the focal point; clean readable digits, no extra text, no UI.

Production: `public/images/theme-pocket/base64-tool.webp`
