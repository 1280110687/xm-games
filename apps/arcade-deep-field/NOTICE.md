XM adaptation retains the upstream deterministic simulation, eight campaign maps, waves, tower upgrades, procedural art and synthesized audio. The network lobby/race/versus modes are excluded. The integration supplies a responsive shell, local language labels, theme bridge and pause handling. No external service is required.
